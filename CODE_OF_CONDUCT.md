# Code of Conduct

Skeleton welcomes technical collaboration conducted in a professional, respectful, and evidence-based manner.

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas and disagreements without harassment or personal attacks;
- critique code, designs, and evidence rather than people;
- respect privacy, security boundaries, and confidential information;
- avoid publishing secrets, personal data, or exploit details;
- keep issue and pull-request discussions relevant to the project;
- follow maintainer requests intended to reduce security, safety, or operational risk.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential disclosure, or attempts to bypass project security controls.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions or discussions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.

For sensitive conduct or security concerns, contact the primary maintainer privately where possible rather than posting private details publicly.
