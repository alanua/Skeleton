# Maintainers

## Primary maintainer

- **@alanua** — project owner and primary maintainer.

The primary maintainer is responsible for architecture direction, issue triage, pull-request review, release/runtime decisions, security-sensitive changes, and final authority over protected or high-risk operations.

## Decision model

Skeleton may use multiple AI systems, runners, and external tools during development, but they are not project maintainers and do not hold independent merge or deployment authority. Human maintainer review remains the final decision boundary for durable code, runtime, security, and physical-action changes.

## Contribution path

Contributors should open an issue or focused pull request and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Security-sensitive reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
